## 4. FIXING METHODS NOT YET ADOPTED

Not defects — options the evidence now argues for, none of which exist in the codebase.

1. **Collapse the class taxonomy.** Given D1, four flat classes are not defensible. Use binary *Coordinated vs Breakdown*, or three classes, as the headline, and keep the 4-class result as the honest negative. This is the largest single lever on producing a number that survives.
2. **Abstention as a first-class output.** For a zero-error-tolerance claim, 0.85 accuracy on 60% of windows with a declared decline rate beats 0.48 on everything. `is_anomaly` already exists — promote it from a flag to a decision, and report the decline rate.
3. **Turn the homography back on.** `court.py` has `build_homography` and `detect_court_quad`; `force_linear` reduces the quad to a mask only. This is why distances are non-metric and why the scale-invariant block had to be invented as a workaround. Fixing it attacks drift at the source. Validate with reprojection error in pixels and recovered court dimensions (should be 18 × 9 m ± 2%); reject and log on failure. It forces re-extraction, so bundle it with that job.
4. **`GroupKFold` by rally** (D4), plus per-video class-prior tables in every report (D1, D2).
5. **Domain-adversarial training** — a small network on the invariant features with a gradient-reversal branch predicting `video_id`. This is the one place a neural net beats HGB here, because trees cannot do adversarial de-biasing. A cheaper alternative is CORAL feature alignment per video.
6. **Ball-channel ablation** — with ball AP50 at 0.543, does the ball feature help at all? Train with and without.
7. **Detector mAP on the three source videos**, not only the 70-image Roboflow validation split — a CV examiner will note those are different distributions.
8. **Sub-component correctness metrics that do not exist at all**: tracking IDF1 / MOTA / ID-switches, team-assignment accuracy against manual labels, court reprojection error, and live-play/replay detection precision-recall. Each one turns "I assert this works" into a measured number.
9. **Inter-annotator κ** — the ceiling. `fyp/annotate.py` implements the blind protocol; it needs a second, ideally volleyball-qualified, annotator for roughly two hours. Interacts with R2.
10. **Ground the taxonomy in literature.** "Coordinated Attack / Delayed Support / Spacing Breakdown" are not standard volleyball constructs. The features already *are* collective-variable measures (hull area = surface area, max-pair distance ≈ stretch index, sync = interpersonal coordination). Citing the team-sport collective-behaviour literature makes the taxonomy principled rather than invented.
