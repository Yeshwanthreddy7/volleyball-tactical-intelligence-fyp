# Coordinated Attack

## What it is
The team turns a good pass into an organised attack: one or two front-court hitters make a fast approach towards the net while the other players hold base positions (setter, back-court cover). In the system it shows as two players moving much faster than the other four, towards the net.

## Signs it is working
- The two attackers start their approach before the set, not after it.
- The remaining players keep their spacing and step in to cover the hitter (attack coverage).
- The team does not bunch around the setter.

## Common faults
- Only one attacker approaches, so the block can stay on one hitter.
- Hitters approach late because the first pass was off target.
- No one covers the hitter, so a blocked ball falls on the floor.

## Coaching drills
- Pass-set-hit triads: passer, setter and two hitters; the coach serves; both hitters must approach on every rep.
- Approach timing drill: hitters start their approach when the setter touches the ball, not when the ball is set.
- Coverage drill: after every attack, the other four players form a cup around the hitter within one second.
