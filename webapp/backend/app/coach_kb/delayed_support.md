# Delayed Support

## What it is
After the ball is played, the nearest player reacts late, so help (cover, second ball, or defence) arrives too late. In the system it shows as one player moving much less than the team while the team moves.

## Why it happens
- The player watches the ball instead of reading the play.
- Poor ready position (standing upright, weight on the heels).
- Unclear responsibility: two players each expect the other to go.
- Fatigue late in a rally or a set.

## Coaching drills
- Ready-position checks: knees bent, weight forward, before every serve and every opponent attack.
- First-step reaction drill: the coach tosses to random spots; the nearest player must take the first step within half a second.
- Calling drill: every ball must be called ("mine") before it crosses the net.
- Conditioning: short interval runs to keep reaction speed late in sets.
