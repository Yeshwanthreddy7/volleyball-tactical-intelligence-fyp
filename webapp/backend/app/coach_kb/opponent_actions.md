# Opponent actions (serve, spike, block)

## Serve
Watch where the opponent serves from and to which zone. If many serves go to one receiver, adjust the reception formation to protect that player.

## Spike
Opponent spikes show where your block and defence must be ready. Count spikes per rotation: a strong opponent hitter needs a double block and a defender behind the block.

## Block
Opponent blocks against your attack mean your hitters are predictable. Vary the set (quick, outside, back-row) and use tips and roll shots over the block.

## How to use the opponent report
Click each time to see the action, then check what your team did in the next one to two seconds (your team's tactic at that moment in the team report).
