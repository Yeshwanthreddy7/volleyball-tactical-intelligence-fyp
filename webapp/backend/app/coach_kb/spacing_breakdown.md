# Spacing Breakdown

## What it is
The formation fails: one player is isolated far from team-mates (a hole in the court), or two players crowd each other (overlap). In the system it shows as a very large gap to the nearest team-mate or two players very close together.

## Why it happens
- A player chases the ball out of their zone and does not return.
- Rotation confusion after a substitution or a lost point.
- Two players go for the same ball (no call).

## Coaching drills
- Zone return drill: after every touch, the player must return to base before the next contact.
- Six-zone floor markers: practise rallies with cones marking each player's base.
- Communication drill: overlapping players must call the ball; the coach rewards clear calls.
- Video review: show the frame where the gap opened and ask who should have covered it.
