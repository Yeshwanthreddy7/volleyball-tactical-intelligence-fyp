# Coordinated Defense

## What it is
The team moves as one rigid unit when the opponent attacks: every player shifts in the same direction by a similar amount, keeping the formation shape. In the system it shows as all players moving together with their gaps staying the same.

## Signs it is working
- All six players shift towards the ball side together.
- Back-row defenders stay balanced in their zones while the front row forms the block.
- Gaps between neighbours stay about the same while the team moves.

## Common faults
- One player moves the opposite way or stays put, breaking the shape (named as "least in step" in the report).
- Defenders move after the attacker hits instead of reading the set.
- The block and the floor defence do not match (a seam opens between them).

## Coaching drills
- Shadow defence: the coach points to an attack zone, all six shift together; repeat until the shape holds.
- Read-the-setter drill: defenders call out where the set goes before it reaches the hitter.
- Block-defence matching: blockers take line or angle, the floor defenders fill the other side.
